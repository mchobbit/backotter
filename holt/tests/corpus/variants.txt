# Golden plans of variants (holt/tests/golden_plans.rs), GUEST+VARIANT a line;
# each is GUEST/plan+VARIANT.txt.  +bigger plans GUEST's dump onto targets
# 1 GiB bigger than its disks, as ht1.sh's bigger variant rebuilds: these are
# testvm/test2/classes.json's holt.variants.bigger.  +oneshot plans as its
# guest does, and +degraded has no dump here, so neither has a plan of its own.
aerynos+bigger
debian13-stack+bigger
mint22+bigger
# +reformat-TYPE plans GUEST's dump with --reformat /=TYPE
# (docs/design/reformat.md): golden-only, not HoltTest2 cells.  A tree
# whose load has no --reformat skips them.  The omarchy ones are
# refusals: it has no xfsprogs (xfs), and it hibernates into a swap file
# on its btrfs (ext4).
debian13+reformat-ext4
endeavouros+reformat-xfs
fedora44+reformat-xfs
omarchy+reformat-ext4
omarchy+reformat-xfs
tumbleweed+reformat-ext4
